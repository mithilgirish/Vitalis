REASONING + ACTING CYCLE

How the Agent Solves Emergencies

1. THOUGHT: Analyze parsed clinical payload (resource, blood group, units, ESI urgency).

2. ACTION (PostGIS): Query candidate facilities within 30km radius holding unexpired stock.

3. OBSERVATION: Evaluate candidate facilities (available units, distance, Medicare quality score).

4. ACTION (XGBoost): Invoke evaluate_stockout_risk to predict donor 72h depletion probability.

5. GUARDRAIL CHECK: Disqualify any facility where Risk_stockout > 0.40 to prevent secondary crash.

6. PRESCRIPTION: Compute Priority Index (PI), rank donor paths, and synthesize reasoning trace.

CUSTOM TOOL ECOSYSTEM

Deterministic Tool Implementations

Tool 1: lookup_candidate_inventory

Executes parameterized PostGIS spatial SQL with ST_Distance and ST_DWithin over WGS84 coordinates. Filters for batches expiring > 24h.

Tool 2: evaluate_stockout_risk

Infers XGBoost binary classifier (xgb_stockout.json) to calculate 72-hour depletion risk. Blocks candidates exceeding 0.40 probability.

Tool 3: calculate_transit_viability

Validates physical route against biological shelf-life constraints: PRBC (35-42 days @ 1-6°C), Platelets (≤5 days agitated), Hearts (≤4h), Kidneys (≤24h).

Mathematical Formulation

The Prescriptive Priority Index (PI)

A multi-objective optimization equation balancing clinical urgency, surplus, spatial transit, depletion risk, and quality penalties.

PRESCRIPTIVE RANKING EQUATION
PI = ( UrgencyScore × SurplusRatio ) / [ Distance(km) × ( 1 + Risk_stockout ) × QualityPenalty ]

Higher PI indicates superior donor optimality. Dynamically optimizes transfer efficiency while mathematically forbidding unsafe drawdowns.

UrgencyScore
6 - Urgency_Tier

ESI 1 (Immediate / Resuscitative) scores 5.
ESI 5 (Non-urgent) scores 1.
Weights clinical life-threat severity.

SurplusRatio
Available / Requested

Ratio of unexpired units held at donor facility to requested units. Rewards facilities with deep inventory cushions.

Risk_stockout
P(Y = 1 | x)

XGBoost 72-hour depletion probability. If Risk > 0.40, facility is hard-disqualified. Penalizes vulnerable donors.

QualityPenalty
1.0 + max(0, ΔQ / 100)

Medicare HVBP/HAC composite score (0-100). Facilities with high infection/complication rates incur algorithmic penalties.

BIOLOGICAL VIABILITY ENVELOPES

Temperature & Time Thresholds

PRBC (Packed Red Blood Cells): 1°C to 6°C | 35-42 days shelf life. Excursion if > 6.0°C; hemolysis occurs rapidly above 10°C.

Platelets: 20°C to 24°C | 5 days maximum. Requires continuous gentle agitation to prevent irreversible aggregation.

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP): ≤ -18°C | 1 year frozen. Thawed at 37°C prior to transfusion.

Solid Organs (Hearts / Lungs): 4°C cold ischemia | ≤ 4 hours surgical window. Zero routing margin.

Solid Organs (Kidneys): 4°C cold ischemia | ≤ 24 hours extended perfusion window.

SUB-2S ALARM PIPELINE

Excursion Detection Dataflow

1. IoT Telemetry Stream: Courier device or BLE smart container transmits temperature & GPS every 15-60s to POST /api/v1/telemetry/log.

2. Backend Verification: Gateway checks reading against product tolerance. If exceeded, sets excursion_flag = TRUE.

3. WebSocket Broadcast (< 1.8s): FastAPI immediately emits critical alarm to all connected command center sessions.

4. Operational Protocol: Flashing red banner and audible buzzer alert coordinator; secondary reroute initiated.

5. Immutable Audit Log: Persisted in transit_telemetry for FDA 21 CFR Part 11 regulatory compliance.
